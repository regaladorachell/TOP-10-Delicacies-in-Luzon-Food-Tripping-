<h2>5. Buko Pie</h2>
<img src="../foodpicture/buko-pie.jpg" alt="Buko Pie" width="300">
<p><b>Place:</b> Laguna</p>
<p>A sweet pie filled with young coconut meat and creamy custard inside a flaky crust. It is a popular pasalubong.</p>
<p><b>Food trip tip:</b> Buy a fresh slice or bring a whole pie home.</p>
