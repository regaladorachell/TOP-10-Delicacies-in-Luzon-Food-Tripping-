<h2>4. Bulalo</h2>
<img src="../foodpicture/bulalo.jpg" alt="Bulalo" width="300">
<p><b>Place:</b> Batangas</p>
<p>A warm beef soup made with beef shank, bone marrow, corn, and vegetables. It is popular during rainy or cool weather.</p>
<p><b>Food trip tip:</b> A comforting meal to share with family or friends.</p>
