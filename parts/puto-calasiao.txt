<h2>7. Puto Calasiao</h2>
<img src="../foodpicture/puto-calasiao.jpg" alt="Puto Calasiao" width="300">
<p><b>Place:</b> Calasiao, Pangasinan</p>
<p>Small, soft, and slightly chewy rice cakes. These sweet treats are commonly sold as snacks or pasalubong.</p>
<p><b>Food trip tip:</b> Enjoy them with coffee or hot chocolate.</p>
