<h2>9. Laing</h2>
<img src="../foodpicture/laing.jpg" alt="Laing" width="300">
<p><b>Place:</b> Bicol Region</p>
<p>Dried taro leaves simmered slowly in coconut milk with chili and seasonings. It has a creamy and savory flavor.</p>
<p><b>Food trip tip:</b> Try it as a side dish with rice.</p>
